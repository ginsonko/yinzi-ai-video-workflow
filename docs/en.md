# Yinzi AI Video Workflow

Yinzi AI Video Workflow is a local-first production application for AI short films and storyboards. It covers script approval, reusable character/scene/prop assets, shot-by-shot planning, optional local 3D previs, first/last-frame continuity, video model routing, narration, subtitles, and final editing.

Windows x64 releases provide both installer and portable executables. Node.js, Python, FFmpeg, and FFprobe are bundled or not required. User projects and settings live under `%APPDATA%\银子AI视频工作流` and are preserved during upgrades and uninstall by default.
